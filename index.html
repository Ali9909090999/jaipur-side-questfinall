Chapter 01 · The beginning
The Paper Plane That Missed Its Flight
A tiny delivery from Mumbai has found its way to Jaipur.
🛩️ Postmark 01 of 04 · Take-off

One small delivery…

Once upon a Wednesday evening, a very determined paper plane tried to book a seat to Jaipur.

Unfortunately, it was informed that the luggage allowance had already been taken up by Madam's essentials: outfits, plans, snacks and an entirely unreasonable amount of nakhre. 😂

So the paper plane took another route. It travelled through the little space between two screens and arrived here instead.

Its message was simple:

“I may not be there in Jaipur, but I can still send a little hello from my side.”

And that, Madam, is how this tiny side quest began.

Before taking off again, the plane dropped a little envelope with four empty postmarks on the front. A note said: “This delivery can only be understood when all four marks have arrived.”

The first clue was tucked underneath: “It cannot be packed in a suitcase, but it can travel with you.”

For now, your only mission is to enjoy the trip. Let the plans happen, collect a few laughs, try something nice and make some memories.

P.S. The paper plane has requested a review of the luggage policy. It feels the nakhre department has too much influence. 😂

But wait… the paper plane says it spotted something pink on the way. It also heard a mysterious “Madam!” being called from somewhere near the postcard stand. Tomorrow may explain both. 👀

The Pink City has a question…

The paper plane has landed safely, and it has sent back a postcard. The postcard is pink, naturally — Jaipur wouldn't have it any other way.

On the back, someone has written:

“The best bits of a trip aren't always on the itinerary. Sometimes they're the tiny, unplanned moments that sneak up on you.”

A tiny reminder from Mumbai 💻

And since the laptop might be travelling with you too… I hope it gets a little holiday. 😂 If something genuinely urgent comes up, maybe give it an hour or so, then let your manager and team handle the rest. You're at the offsite to enjoy Jaipur too — not to run the whole office from there. 😊

Madam's little care note 🌷

Please look after yourself while you're away, okay? Have proper meals (don't survive on snacks and coffee alone 😂), take your medicines on time, and try to get good sleep even if a new place makes it a little difficult. And go a little easy on cold things, especially while you're travelling.

Basically: eat well, rest well and take care of yourself. Let the usual work worries sit on mute for a couple of days, enjoy yourself, laugh lots and come back feeling refreshed. That's my main wish for you. ❤️

A tiny request from Mumbai 📸

And Madam… if you feel like it, send me a picture of yourself now and then — or a little glimpse of something you spot in Jaipur. Hehe. I may not be there with you, but seeing a tiny bit of your day from here would make me feel a little more connected. No pressure to keep sending updates, though. Enjoy your trip first. 😊

Which brings us to a very important, highly scientific question for Madam.

Chapter 04 · The little ending
A Note for the Journey Back
The last little delivery has reached its destination.
💌 Postmark 04 of 04 · Delivered

And that's the little side quest. ✨

So, Madam, the paper plane has completed its journey. It has collected a postcard, survived the Level Department and made it through the Jaipur side quest in one piece. 😂

But the thing it was carrying all along wasn't a secret object or a grand surprise.

Remember the first clue? Something that couldn't be packed in a suitcase, but could still travel with you.

It was a little thought, a shared joke, a small hello — the kind of thing that can cross the distance between Mumbai and Jaipur without needing a boarding pass.

And the four postmarks? They were simply four little chances to make an ordinary trip feel a bit more personal.

I couldn't be there in Jaipur, so I made this tiny corner from my side of the screen. A little story, a few silly references and a few moments that I hoped would make you smile while you were away.

And there's one little thing I wanted to say before this page signs off: I made it because I wanted to be a small part of your days, even from a distance. Just a few little hellos from my side, hoping they'd find you at the right moment and make you smile. That's really all there is to it.

Also, Jaipur may be the Pink City, but you've got your own way of making things a little brighter. And yes, Madam, you're pretty special to me. ❤️

I hope the trip gave you some lovely memories — the planned ones, the random ones, and the ones that become funny stories later.

Have a safe journey home, Madam. Take your time, enjoy the rest of the day, and come back with at least one story that deserves a full dramatic retelling. 😂

P.S. The Nakhre Department remains open. There is no retirement plan. ❤️
